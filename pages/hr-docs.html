Accepted Values & Departments

Department

The department field accepts any free-text string. There is no fixed enumeration — enter the department name exactly as your organization uses it. Values are case-sensitive in grouping on the Payroll Register.

Common examples used at EPC Commercial Corporation: Operations, Admin, Sales, Warehouse, Finance.

Working Days Per Month

Accepted values: any positive integer from 1 to 31. Blank or missing defaults to 26.

This value is the denominator in all proration formulas. Common values:

ValueMeaning
22Weekdays only (approx.)
24Mon–Sat schedule
26Standard (default)
306 days/week, full month

Allowance Amounts (Meal, Transportation, Other)

All allowance fields accept a non-negative number in Philippine pesos. In the CSV, enter the raw number (e.g. 2000 or 2000.50). Comma-formatted values and the ₱ symbol are also accepted by the parser. Blank or 0 means no allowance of that type.

Deduct allowance on absence NEW

This setting controls whether an employee's allowances are reduced when they are absent. It can be set per employee — individually on the employee form, or in bulk via the CSV column deduct_allowance_on_absence.

Where to find it (individual form)

On the Add / Edit Employee page, open any employee record. Scroll to the "Absence & Allowance Settings" section at the bottom of the form. Toggle the "Deduct allowances on absence" switch to YES or NO and save.

CSV column

Column NameAccepted ValuesBlank / Missing
deduct_allowance_on_absence YES or NO (case-insensitive) Defaults to NO

Behavior for each value

YES Prorate allowances
Each allowance (meal, transportation, other) is multiplied by the fraction days present ÷ working days per month. An employee absent for 2 days out of 26 receives 24/26 of each allowance.
NO Full allowances always
All allowances are released in full regardless of the number of absences. Only the basic salary is prorated for absences (standard practice).

Proration formula (when YES)

allowance_paid = (allowance_amount ÷ working_days_per_month) × days_present
Applied independently to each allowance field: meal, transportation, and other.
working_days_per_month is taken from the employee's own record (default: 26).

Worked example

Example
Meal allowance: ₱2,500 / month
Working days per month: 26
Days absent: 2  →  Days present: 24
allowance_paid = (₱2,500 ÷ 26) × 24
= ₱96.1538… × 24
= ₱2,307.69
Note: The Working Days Per Month field on each employee record is the denominator for this formula. Ensure it is set accurately per employee before running payroll. Changing it after payroll computation will affect future runs but not already-generated registers.

CSV Import Notes

General Rules

The importer auto-detects column positions from the header row. Column names are matched case-insensitively. Extra or unknown columns are ignored silently. Rows where name is blank are skipped.

After upload, all rows are shown in an editable batch table where every cell can be modified before saving. The deduct_allowance_on_absence column is exposed as a YES/NO dropdown in the batch table.

Download the Template

Use the CSV Template button on the Employees page to download a pre-formatted template with all required columns and two sample rows. The last column in the template is deduct_allowance_on_absence.
